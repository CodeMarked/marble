## When to decide (vs keep reading)

- **Default:** treat each row as **decide-by-book-chunk**, not calendar time. The **target decision gate** is the chunk where the textbook gives enough context to choose without guessing (see `[book/chunks/index.md](../book/chunks/index.md)`).
- **Solve earlier only** when implementation is already in progress and an answer blocks correct code (e.g. you are merging a render path this month and need a boundary call). Otherwise prefer **deferring** until the listed chunk so decisions stay evidence-backed.
- **Optional now:** small, reversible follow-ups that do not lock architecture (e.g. expose existing simulation counters in diagnostics) can ship anytime; they do not require closing these rows.

## Current roadmap (blocking vs track)

For the **next engineering focus** in [`MASTER_PLAN.md`](../MASTER_PLAN.md) (**shader pipeline**, **RHI evolution**, resource manager / ACP), treat **Milestone role = Blocking (shader/RHI)** and **Blocking (render/assets)** rows as **blocking** until decided or explicitly deferred with recorded rationale (see **Recorded deferrals** below): **shader compilation** (long-term story vs ADR-0057 baseline), **`VulkanRhi` thickness** vs game-specific binding (ADR-0056 baseline), and—when expanding **asset conditioning / packs**—**intermediate asset formats and ACP stages**. When targeting **external SDK adoption** as in [`architecture/sdk-and-samples-roadmap.md`](../architecture/sdk-and-samples-roadmap.md), treat **public C++ API and versioning** (**Blocking (SDK-ready)**) as **blocking** for a **stable integrator** milestone unless explicitly deferred with rationale.

**Chunk-gated follow-through (not blocking shader/RHI/ACP by default):** engine vs gameplay boundary, fixed-step stall policy, concurrency default, data ownership, profiling gates, `docs/` publication policy, and vcpkg vs Vulkan SDK onboarding — each defers to its **Target decision gate** column unless a release milestone forces an early cut, per [`guardrails/architecture-guardrails.md`](../guardrails/architecture-guardrails.md). Verdict detail per row: [`OPEN_QUESTIONS_ADR_CRITIQUE.md`](OPEN_QUESTIONS_ADR_CRITIQUE.md).

**Proceeding on baselines:** Until superseding ADRs land, engineering may rely on accepted baselines: **[ADR-0057](../decisions/ADR-0057-shader-compilation-baseline.md)** (offline `glslc`, staged SPIR-V), **[ADR-0056](../decisions/ADR-0056-rhi-submission-seam-and-irenderbackend.md)** (submission seam / `IRenderBackend`), and **[ADR-0024](../decisions/ADR-0024-runtime-resource-registry-and-lifetime-baseline.md)** (resource manager contract) — aligned with **Baseline** / **Defer** verdicts in the critique cross-walk. “Blocking” here means **a decision is required before expanding past those baselines** (e.g. libshaderc, hot reload, thick RHI, full ACP), not that all work must stop.

## Recorded deferrals (engineering may proceed)

- **Shaders and RHI:** Current samples (including **`marbles`**) continue **ADR-0057** (`glslc` + staged SPIR-V) and **ADR-0056** (submission seam; `VulkanRhi` implements `IRenderBackend`) until a listed trigger forces a new decision: **second graphics backend**, **shader hot reload**, **ACP milestone** requiring different conditioning, or explicit maintainer cut to adopt **libshaderc** / checked-in SPIR-V / thicker RHI split.
- **Assets:** Runtime registry + search root + `ResourceKind` (ADR-0024 baseline) may advance without a final ACP intermediate-format answer until **packs/importers** or **typed mesh/texture loaders** require locking formats.
- **Physics, universe, multiplayer, and other lanes** may proceed in parallel per [`MASTER_PLAN.md`](../MASTER_PLAN.md) §1 and §4; they must not silently violate **Closed** or **Blocking** rows without an ADR or an update to this file.
